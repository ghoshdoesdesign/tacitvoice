# TacitVoice: project notes

Silent dictation prototype for macOS (PRD working name "Murmur"). One user, research use.

## Requirements (do not regress)
- Hold-to-talk only: camera and microphone run only while right Option is held.
- No video or audio is written to disk outside `tools/record.py`. Video never leaves the device. The owner allowed audio to go to the cloud for speed: with an OpenAI key, each utterance's audio goes to OpenAI for speech-to-text (`TACIT_STT=local` keeps it on device). Text goes out for the cleanup pass.
- The server listens on 127.0.0.1 only and requires the token in `~/.tacitvoice/token`.
- Take ideas from github.com/evanhu1/telepathy, but do not copy its code (it has no licence).
- Auto-AVSR code and weights stay out of the repo (`third_party/` is gitignored; research-only licence).

## Layout
- `server/`: local FastAPI server (`main.py`). The models run in a separate process on its main thread (`worker.py`): inside the web server's process recognition stalled and the process vanished without an error, while a plain process works. The worker is restarted if it dies or takes over 45 s. `pipeline.py` runs `stt.py` (OpenAI transcription API, or local Whisper) and `lips.py` (Auto-AVSR, n-best) in parallel, then `phrases.py` (glossary and past prompts) and `cleanup.py` (OpenAI or Claude, chosen by `TACIT_CLEANUP_PROVIDER` or the key present; structured output; falls back to the raw transcript on any failure).
- `tools/record.py`, `tools/evaluate.py`: record labelled clips and measure word error rate and latency.
- `sentences/calibration.txt`, `tools/pack.py`, `train/`: personal fine-tuning. Record `calibration.txt` with `tools.record --out data/train` and `calibration2.txt` with `--out data/train2`; `tools.pack` cuts them to mouth crops in `data/pack.npz` (train = `data/train` + `data/train2`, test = `data/baseline`), and `train/finetune_colab.ipynb` trains on a Colab GPU. First run, 41 min of data: silent lip error on the test prompts fell from 65.2% to 50.3% (47.9% after cleanup). Second run, 75 min: 47.3% (46.1% after cleanup), so more short sentences give little. The notebook embeds `train/finetune.py`; after editing the script run `python train/build_notebook.py` (a test checks they match). Tuned weights go in `~/.tacitvoice/lips_finetuned.pth` and are picked up automatically.
- `collect/`: a self-contained recording page for other people. `page.html` is the template, `build.py` embeds the sentences and the settings in `settings.json` and writes `index.html` (a test checks they match). It records video only, never sound, and keeps takes in the browser's IndexedDB. With `upload_url` set (the owner asked for this) "Send your recordings" posts each clip to the owner's Google Drive through `apps_script.gs`, a receiver the owner deploys from their own Google account; that address is the only place the page may send to. With it empty the page offers a .zip download and sends nothing. `tools/import_friend.py` unpacks a person's Drive folder, Drive's zip of it, or the page's own zip into `data/friends/<name>/` (the input is untrusted: only `NNNN_<mode>.(webm|mp4)` entries named in its manifest are read). `tools.pack` includes everyone under `data/friends` with a `speaker` field, and `finetune.py --holdout-speaker NAME` tests on a person left out of training.
- `app/`: Swift menu-bar app (SwiftPM, bundled by `scripts/build_app.sh`). Hotkey, capture, pill, paste-insert. The interface sits at the bottom centre of the screen, every state centred on the same point. With the owner's animations installed there is no text in the working states: a morphing orb while the key is held (`~/.tacitvoice/orb/`, from `tools/make_orb.py`), the orb with circling dots after release (`orb-loading/`, opening plays once then `loop.txt` onwards repeats), and the orb closing when the text lands (`orb-done/`, both from `tools/make_loading.py`). The videos are the owner's and are not in the repo. Without a set, that state falls back to a black label ("Observing lip movement…", "Polishing your thoughts…", "Inserted"). Errors and "No words detected" always use the label.
- `tests/`: pytest; models are faked. `uv run pytest`.

## Conventions
- `/transcribe` is multipart: `meta` JSON, `audio` raw 16-bit mono PCM, `frames` JPEGs in order.
- Frames are resampled to 25 fps by timestamp before lip reading.
- A clip counts as silent mouthing, and skips speech-to-text (it invents text on silence), when its loudest 100 ms is under `TACIT_SILENCE_DBFS` or less than `TACIT_MIN_ACTIVITY` of it rises above its own noise floor. Loudness alone does not separate the two in the owner's room. Stock phrases such as "Thank you." are also discarded.
- Cloud speech-to-text uses `whisper-1`; `gpt-4o-transcribe` truncates and paraphrases whispered audio.
- Lips are read only when no whisper is heard (`TACIT_LIPS_WITH_AUDIO=on` reads them always). Measured on 20 whispered clips: 17.9% word error from audio alone, 17.6% with lips, at 3-4 s extra.
- The lip model always outputs a sentence, even for a still mouth. `lips.mouth_motion` measures how much the mouth opened and closed (variation over the clip of the brightness at the mouth's centre); a silent clip under `TACIT_LIPS_MIN_MOTION` (1.5) is not read and the app shows "No words detected". The measure was chosen by `tools.motion`, which scores candidates on recorded clips using the pauses at the start and end of each as the still case: on 169 of the owner's clips it got 4% of pauses and 4% of sentences wrong at its best cutoff, while measures of frame-to-frame movement got 10-22% of pauses wrong. It is in grey levels and tested on one person and webcam; `tools.motion` also reports a contrast-normalised variant to check on other people's recordings.
- Lip reading runs on the CPU: Auto-AVSR's beam search fails on Apple's GPU (mps) with a device mismatch.
- `tools.evaluate` ignores `~/.tacitvoice/prompts.txt` unless `--known-prompts`, so scores measure recognition, not lookup.
- The Swift app cannot be built on Linux; changes to it need a build on the Mac.
