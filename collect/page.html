Help teach a computer to read lips

A research project on silent speech. About __MINUTES__ minutes. You can stop at any time and still send what you recorded.

What you will do

  • Read short sentences from the screen, each one three ways: aloud, whispered, and mouthed with no sound.
  • Your camera records video of you while you say each one. No sound is recorded.
  • At the end you download one file and send it to the person who gave you this link.

Face the camera with light on your face. Keep your whole mouth in view.

Say it aloud

Or hold Space while you say it, and let go when you finish.

Thank you. One last step

Send the downloaded file to the person who gave you this link. It is large, so use AirDrop, Google Drive, WeTransfer or similar.

After they confirm they have it, you can remove the recordings from this browser.
